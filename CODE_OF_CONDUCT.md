# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as maintainers and contributors of `helio-safety-gate` pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, patient-safety-first experience for everyone—regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, language, nationality, religion, or socioeconomic background.

## Expected Behavior

- Prioritize patient safety, radical honesty about system limitations, and respect for local clinicians and working-class patients.
- Communicate constructively across technical, clinical, and linguistic backgrounds (English, Tamil, and other Indian languages are welcome).
- Protect privacy: never share real patient records or identifiable personal details in public threads.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or privacy-violating behavior may be reported to `haroon@heonixai.in`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
